Privacy & limitations

Know what leaves your browser.

  • Page text is sent only when you submit a prompt.
  • When asking about a page, its text may be sent to your configured AI provider.
  • API keys are stored in local extension storage and are not encrypted by ORBIT.
  • Some providers block browser-origin requests. Use a provider that supports CORS or a trusted proxy you control.
  • Never send secrets or private data unless you trust the provider.
